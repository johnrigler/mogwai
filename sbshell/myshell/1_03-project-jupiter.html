What is known and what remains open

Oracle identifies itself as a planned tenant of Project Jupiter, with infrastructure being built for OpenAI. Its published plans distinguish construction from later operation. Court and environmental permitting records are public sources for the project's regulatory history. Financial headlines are evidence of reported claims, not the contracts themselves.

Missing primary source. Reporting says Oracle sent a force majeure notice connected to the project in September 2026. The notice and the executed agreements have not been obtained for this dossier. A private contractual notice is not necessarily a court filing.

The missing meter

Operating telemetry is not evidence yet. With operator authorization, future CPU figures could come from host-level tools such as vmstat, and accelerator readings from GPU-management interfaces. A host sample is not a campus-wide utilization measurement. No actual Project Jupiter CPU/GPU samples are part of this record; values are null, not measured zeros.

Construction progress, theoretical compute capacity, nameplate megawatts, metered electricity and GPU use must remain distinct measurement classes.
